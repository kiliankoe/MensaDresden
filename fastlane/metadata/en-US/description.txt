See what's for lunch (or dinner) at Dresden's canteens today. Ideal for any hungry student at TU Dresden, HTW Dresden or other universities and schools in the city.

Need to know how much money is still left on your Emeal? Just scan it. Additionally you can enter your Autoload credentials and have the app show you a list of your recent transactions.

Be sure to check the settings to note any allergies or unwanted ingredients so as to find your favorite meal even quicker.
